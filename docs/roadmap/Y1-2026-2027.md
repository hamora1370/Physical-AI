# Year 1 — robot engineering foundations

**Window:** 2026-10-23 to 2027-10-22. **Guide:** 5 focused hours/week.
**Year exit:** a demonstrated and measured safe, low-power manipulation task, with fundamental kinematics, software stack and failure analysis documented.

| Quarter | Window (approx.) | Learning focus | Required demonstration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 2026-10-26 → 2027-01-24 | SCARA-01 compact printable R-R-P-R as **robot-learning-ready** platform: requirements, 2R FK/IK, mechanisms, measured-state/gripper feedback design, ROS 2 and simulation | tested FK/IK, mechanical/controller concept, draft command/observations + episode schema, camera provision, simulated R-R-P pick/place; optional guarded 2R prototype |
| Q2 | 2027-01-25 → 2027-04-25 | SCARA-01 safe staged hardware: encoders, homing, gripper observability, low-level control, ROS state streams and timestamped telemetry | guarded low-energy physical motion, demonstrable measured-vs-commanded state, calibrated faults/limits and first hardware episode logs |
| Q3 | 2027-04-26 → 2027-07-25 | SCARA-01 motion planning/collision diagnostics, camera mounting and calibration when hardware is ready | simulated obstacle planning, reproducible camera-to-base calibration or documented reasons to defer |
| Q4 | 2027-07-26 → 2027-10-22 | Vision-assisted SCARA-01 pick/place; time-correlated episodes and evaluation data quality | measured task completion on defined objects, classical baseline and optional small demonstration-dataset pilot (no trained policy required) |

## Required theory

- homogeneous transformations and frame chaining;
- FK / numeric IK, including unreachable configurations;
- Jacobian basics and singularity awareness;
- feedback concepts, trajectory profiles, actuator constraints;
- collision-free planning vs execution;
- safe bench operations and independent stops.

## Boundaries

A leader/follower educational arm can be added after Q1 if procurement is justified. Do not assume a particular open-source arm has out-of-the-box support for MoveIt or ros2_control.

## Evidence

- Explain each concept from a blank page.
- At least one executable example with automated tests per major math topic.
- Simulation demo including launch/run steps, measured results and limitations.
- Failures documented with root cause hypotheses.

**Design choice:** [SCARA-01 engineering concept](../projects/SCARA-01-CONCEPT.md) and [robot-learning-ready contract](../projects/SCARA-01-LEARNING-PLATFORM.md) as ratified by [ADR-0002](../decisions/ADR-0002-scara-learning-platform.md). Q1 physical construction is optional; the minimum gate is validated engineering + reproducible simulation. Q2 emphasizes stepwise safe physical integration. This changes the teaching vehicle, not the five-year mission.
