### Week 1 exit criteria

1. Explain Node vs Process vs Thread vs Executor in a fresh robotic example, including Callback Group effects.
2. Explain why multi-threading does not necessarily mean parallel CPU execution (particularly in ordinary CPython).
3. Show at least one measured Timer callback interval distribution and identify a credible source of Jitter.
4. Explain `header.stamp` and when tf2 should use the measurement-time transform; identify latency and clock limitations.
5. Save a concise learning note with actual observed outputs, unresolved questions, and one independent explanation. Update skill level only after assessment evidence is reviewed.
