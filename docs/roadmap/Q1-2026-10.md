# Q1 2026 — 13-week project-based robotics learning plan (SCARA-01)

**Updated:** 2026-10-26 · **Window:** Mon 2026-10-26 → Sun 2027-01-24 · **Budget:** ~65 focused hours (~5/week).

**Curriculum decision:** robotics engineering and ROS 2 are taught together against a single low-energy, desktop **SCARA-01 3-DOF R-R-P** project. The teaching order is **physical problem → mechanics/math/control theory → calculation/experiment → ROS 2/software implementation → learner explanation/evidence**. Neither a ROS 2 software-only track nor theory without application is the main path.

**Authoritative project concept:** [SCARA-01 engineering brief](../projects/SCARA-01-CONCEPT.md).

## Q1 outcomes / mandatory exit gates

1. Define and justify SCARA-01's small-part tray-to-tray pick-and-place task, workcell layout, geometry, payload assumptions, motion limits and basic independent safety concepts.
2. Independently derive and numerically test 2R FK and IK (both branches, joint-limited/unreachable cases) and demonstrate SCARA R-R-P position model. Test Jacobian/velocity or singularity cases.
3. Produce a parametric mechanical concept and quantified initial torque/speed/load/stiffness and actuator/feedback tradeoffs; **no unvalidated purchase or promise of payload accuracy**.
4. Build a reproducible model and simulated 3-DOF tray-to-tray sequence with URDF/Xacro, ROS 2 state/frame observation and recorded outcomes, or explicitly document simulator blockers with a reproducible kinematic fallback. Establish a clear pass/fail definition.
5. Produce a reviewed Q1 engineering report, learning evidence and a justified Q2 plan.

**Stretch only:** safely controlled low-voltage 2R physical benchtop prototype (potentially Z/gripper if justified). A fully built industrial 3-axis machine is **not** a Q1 commitment. Hardware purchasing and motion require appropriate mechanism calculations, guarding, limits, an independent stop and supervision. ROS 2 is not the sole safety mechanism.

## 13-week roadmap

| Weeks (dates) | Central engineering questions / teaching | Measurable SCARA-01 result |
|---|---|---|
| **1–2 (Oct 26–Nov 8)** | Use case, R/R/P joints and DoF, engineering requirements, ROS 2 basics already explored; coordinate frames and workcell safety | Requirements v0; task sequence; sketch, axes and proposed workspace; assessment of prior ROS/Executor labs |
| **3–4 (Nov 9–22)** | 2R geometry, transforms, workspace, FK and IK; link the previously studied Base/Wrist/Tool frames to SCARA | Independently derived equations; known/unknown target tests; joint limits and two elbow branches |
| **5–6 (Nov 23–Dec 6)** | Structures, materials, actuators, transmission, payload, inertia, stiffness, backlash and preliminary sizing | Parametric CAD concept; torque/speed/deflection envelopes; actuator tradeoff; parts list candidate (not an order) |
| **7–8 (Dec 7–20)** | Encoders, homing, feedback, speed/current limits, control scheduling and fault states; connect prior Executor/Jitter discussion to robot motion | Control block diagram; sensor/driver plan; guarded optional 2R bench checks; explicit stop/limit strategy |
| **9–10 (Dec 21–Jan 3)** | Jacobian, singularity, velocity/acceleration, motion profiles and reachability tests; introduce Z control | Time-parameterized R-R-P point-to-point sequence; tests for limits, unreasonable targets and smoothness |
| **11–12 (Jan 4–17)** | URDF/Xacro, tf2 and timestamps, ROS 2 coordination, simulation and repeatable pick/place execution | Visible 3-DOF model, joint states, coherent frame tree and documented simulated tray transfer; optional physical extension |
| **13 (Jan 18–24)** | Review measured outcomes, failure modes, learning assessment and Q2 physical build priorities | Replayable demo or documented kinematic fallback, tests/notes, gaps and Q2 backlog |

Software is used as an engineering instrument *within* these tasks. Services/Actions, deeper QoS/DDS, Motion Planning/MoveIt 2 and the choice of low-level control interface are learned when their capabilities solve a SCARA-01 problem. Do not install infrastructure to complete a checklist. Vision AI and ERP integration are later expansions, not mandatory Q1 deliverables.

## Week 1 — adaptive plan (Oct 26–Nov 1)

**Primary objective:** transition from isolated ROS exercises to robot system design, without discarding or repeating earlier progress.

| Session | Budget | Topic | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| A | 60 min | SCARA vs 2R vs 3R; choose R-R-P; roles of J1/J2/Z and the gripper; draw end-to-end pick/place | Explain 3 positioning DOF and why the gripper's actuation is separate |
| B | 75 min | Define tray geometry, payload class, end-effector grasp, reachable locations, accuracy vs repeatability | Requirements v0 with uncertainties and measurable test method |
| C | 75 min | Sketch 2R geometry and estimate ideal radial reach for candidate `L1=180 mm`, `L2=140 mm`; introduce joint limits and collisions | Labelled workcell and workspace calculation (40–320 mm ideal envelope, *not* usable reach) |
| D | 60 min | Link existing ROS 2/Executor experiments to SCARA architecture: motor interface, joint state publisher, planner and transform chain | One clear node/process/controller responsibility diagram; distinguish test execution from proven timing |
| E | 30 min | Independent conceptual recap and prioritized next decisions | Brief notes, open risks and Week 2 next step |

**Total:** 5 hours. Exact days are flexible. Don't re-teach introductory 2D FK or rerun concurrency labs only to satisfy a checklist.

### Week 1 exit check

- Can sketch SCARA R-R-P, define each joint variable, distinguish gripper from positioning DoF and explain why the system is initially only 3 positioning DoF.
- Can compute the unconstrained radial envelope and explain why real limits reduce it.
- Can justify at least three candidate requirements (part dimensions/mass, tray geometry, accuracy/repeatability, reach or Z travel) while marking unmeasured values as estimates.
- Can explain ROS 2 vs local controller responsibilities and identify one failure/safety scenario.
- A concise note captures design assumptions and unknowns. No premature assessment of professional engineering competency.

## Work model / evidence

Maintain **~2 hours engineering theory, 2 hours problem-solving/CAD/code/hands-on and 1 hour analysis/notes each week**. Limits are ~5 hours total; changes in pace are driven by comprehension and hardware readiness, not a list of software tools. Keep one theory issue + one implementation issue active at a time.

For learning claims record independent explanation, known conventions, actual numerical tests, reproducible commands/artifacts and limitations. `L0` means unassessed, not incapable. Reference [skill matrix](../skills/SKILL_MATRIX.csv) and [evidence protocol](../EVIDENCE_PROTOCOL.md). Public repository: no company secrets, credentials or private factory media.
